# Task Plan: GB-200 — Staged B crate strategy

> **Kind:** Epic **Status:** Planned **Decision date:** 2026-10-29
> **Phases:** GB-199 → GB-201 → GB-202 → Phase A (GB-131, GB-189, GB-130) → GB-203 → GB-204

## 1. The decision

Three strategies were on the table after the GB-194/GB-198 audit produced facts rather than
impressions:

| | What it buys | Why it was not chosen |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **A** Foundation only | `gaugeblock-git` + `gaugeblock-workspace`; mesh frozen by a gate | Leaves the six lateral edges and a 15.8k-LOC `miter-engine` in place, so the two largest measured problems stay unfixed |
| **B (chosen)** Foundation, then `miter-engine` split, then de-mesh | Fixes the two concentrated problems in an order where every intermediate state ships | — |
| **C** Consolidate workspaces | One lockfile, trivial cross-crate refactors | **Law 2 holds.** The eleven repositories stay standalone and independently publishable. C is rejected for this program, not deferred |

Because Law 2 holds, no phase may create a hard cross-repo build requirement. Foundation crates ship
from `common/` on their own cadence and consumers declare a minimum version. `.cargo/config.toml`
`[patch.crates-io]` stays a developer convenience, never a product dependency.

## 2. Fact base (measured 2026-10-29, miter 0.1.0-beta.4)

| Measurement | Command | Value |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Files over the 250 ceiling | `miter audit --path . --threshold 250` | **0** |
| Files in the 200-250 band | `miter audit --path . --threshold 200` | **70** - engine 21, benchwork root 9, storypole-core 5, storage 4, mcp 4 |
| Crates / import edges | `miter deps --path .` | **42 / 132** |
| Declared but unreferenced | `miter deps --path .` | **59** over nine repositories |
| `git` wrapper call sites | `grep Command::new("git")` | **66** across 24 files in outfeed, miter, storypole |
| Structural clones >= 0.90 | `miter clones` | 100 pairs, 17 cross-crate, shape-only on spot check (GB-197) |

In-degree tells the story: `gaugeblock-contracts` 17 in / 0 out is a proper foundation leaf;
`storypole-core` 17, `dado-core` 13, `sawhorse-core` 13, `miter-engine` 12 and `chalkline-core` 11 are
all hubs that also depend outward. That is a mesh, and only six of its edges are lateral:

| From | To | Sites |
|:--|:--|--:|
| `batten-core` | `dado-core` | 7 |
| `chalkline-core` | `miter-engine` | 12 |
| `chalkline-core` | `storypole-core` | 6 |
| `dado-core` | `chalkline-core` | 3 |
| `dado-core` | `miter-engine` | 2 |
| `chalkline-core` | `sawhorse-core` | 2 |

Two caveats are part of the fact base, not footnotes: `miter deps` currently counts test-fixture
strings as references (GB-199), and `miter clones` cannot yet separate structure from duplication
(GB-197). Neither number may drive a gate until the first is fixed.

## 3. Phases

### Phase 0 - make the numbers trustworthy (GB-199)
`miter deps` must stop counting raw-string fixtures and `#[cfg(test)]` bodies as proof of use.
**Exit:** two consecutive runs agree, and `miter-engine -> outfeed` / `-> storypole-core` are gone
from the graph.

### Phase 1a - purge the dead declarations (GB-201)
Baseline 59: batten 9, dado 9, rabbet 9, benchwork 7, chalkline 7, joiner 5, outfeed 5, sawhorse 4,
storypole 3. Remove what the scan cannot justify; keep a declaration only with a written reason
(derive-only use, feature enablement, build script).
**Exit:** `miter deps --path . --unused` is zero, with per-repo before/after recorded here and in the
commits.

### Phase 1b - freeze the mesh (GB-202)
`miter deps --layers` classifies every edge as foundation / domain / integrator, and `--check` fails on
a lateral domain edge or on any edge missing from `.gaugeblock/miter/layers-allowlist.toml`. The six
edges above seed that file, each entry carrying its ticket key, so day one is green and every entry is
tracked debt.

**Open decision, recommendation included:** wire `--check` into the Tier 3 gate (`outfeed gate`) rather
than per-repo CI, because the layer map is a suite property that no single repo can evaluate.

### Phase A - the no-regret foundation (GB-131, GB-189, GB-130)
* `gaugeblock-git` - 66 call sites across 24 files. Owns `common_dir`/worktree detection, worktree
  lifecycle, ref ranges, scoped staging and push. Consumers delete their wrappers; they do not
  re-export two copies.
* `gaugeblock-workspace` - `.gaugeblock/` resolution derived from the git common dir, 11-repo topology,
  `SuiteConfig`. Replaces the ~10-site derivation spread across storypole plus chalkline, dado, joiner.
* `gaugeblock-mcp` - evaluate, do not assume (GB-130): seven MCP servers duplicate JSON-RPC stdio
  boilerplate. Measure the duplicated surface first; a thin dispatch shim justifies a crate, tool
  schemas do not.

**Exit:** wrapper families deleted, path resolution has one owner, MCP decision recorded with its
measurement.

### Phase B1 - split `miter-engine` (GB-203)
15,776 LOC, 21 of the 70 band files, 12 dependents, four unrelated jobs:

| Target crate | Contents | Consumer evidence |
|:---|:---|:---|
| `miter-polyglot` | language visitors, grammars, markdown/html/typescript, syntax diagnostics | `miter-ast` already owns grammars; GB-157 wants this seam |
| `miter-inventory` | cargo_parser, clap_extractor, git_extractor, public_types_extractor, toolchain_extractor | used by audit, deps, doc generators |
| `miter-analysis` | audit, clones, contracts, cycles, deps, hygiene, impact, references, skeleton, slice | the heavy half, where GB-159's features belong |
| `miter-engine` | facade re-exporting the three | no consumer path may change |

Constraints: no new crate over roughly 5k LOC, no file over 250 lines, `cargo nextest --workspace`
green at every commit. The 21 band files are swept inside the series, not afterwards.

**Sequencing note:** extract `security` (GB-158) *before* this split. `joiner-core` depends on
`miter-engine` solely for `scrub_source_text`, so it is a rehearsal on a one-function surface and it
drops `miter-engine`'s in-degree from 12 to 11.

### Phase B2 - burn the six edges (GB-204)
Per call site choose exactly one treatment and record it:

* **(a)** move shared data types into `gaugeblock-contracts` / `gaugeblock-workspace`;
* **(b)** depend on the lower-layer `miter-polyglot` instead of the whole analysis stack;
* **(c)** invert control behind a trait or CLI boundary.

An edge leaves the allowlist only when its site count reaches zero, so partial progress never silences
the gate. **Exit:** allowlist empty, layers check green with no exceptions.

## 4. Measurement discipline (law 12)

Every phase writes before/after into its commit, its ticket, and the table in section 2. A phase that
finds no improvement says so. The two numbers to watch: band files (70 today) and lateral edges (6).

## 5. Non-goals

* No repo consolidation, no shared version train, no renames of published crate names.
* No gate on the 200-250 band - that is GB-195, advisory and per-repo.
* No new `common/` crate without a measured call-site count behind it.
* No temporary re-export of a wrapper a foundation crate was meant to replace.

## 6. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|:--|:--|
| Feature unification: optional deps in `miter-engine` resolve for all consumers at once | Physical split first (B1), features second (GB-159) |
| Allowlist rot becomes permanent permission | Every entry carries a ticket; B2 exits only with an empty file |
| Two crates wrap the same thing during migration | Phase A exit requires deletion, not delegation |
| A phase stalls and blocks the rest | Phase 1b freezes growth, so a stall costs progress, not correctness |
